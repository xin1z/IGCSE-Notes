> Why is water important?
> Water is an important solvent.
> Metabolic reactions take place if chemicals are dissolved.
> &rarr; Transport, excretion

### Different molecules

#### Carbohydrates

Contain: carbon, hydrogen, oxygen

Starch, cellulose, glycogen, sugars etc. etc.

#### Sugars

Taste sweet

Soluble in water

E.g. glucose, sucrose

#### Glucose

Used in respiration to release energy

Dissolved in plasma and transported around body

Excess glucose is converted to glycogen

Sucrose is transported in plants

#### Polysaccharide

Many sugars join together 

Glycogen in animal cells as energy store
starch in plants as energy store
cellulose in plant cell as cell wall

#### Lipid / Fats

Contain: carbon, hydrogen, oxygen

One glycerol and three fatty acids
as energy store

Adipose tissue

Long term energy store
as heat insulation
as cushion
make cell membrane

#### Proteins

Contain: carbon, hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen

Made of long chains of amino acids

> 20 different amino acids
> Different sequences of amino acids give different shapes to protein molecules
> Carrier protein for transport across membrane 
> Enzymes to speed up reactions
> Antibody to help kill bacteria and viruses
> Hemoglobin to transport oxygen

### DNA

Made of **nucleotide**

A double helix with two strands
each of them made of nucleotides

Two strands crosslink and coil together to make a double helix

Contains A, T, C, G

> Complementary base pairing 
> A pairs with T
> C pairs with G

> Carry genetic information
> the sequence of bases determines the sequence of amino acids
> Which determines:
> Body structure
> How metabolic reactions take place

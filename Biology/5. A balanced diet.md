A diet contains all required nutrients
in suitable proportions
with the right amount of energy

### Nutrients

#### Vitamin C

Make protein e.g. collagen and keep tissue in good repair

Deficiency causes scurvy
&rarr; bleeding gum, pain in joints and muscles

#### Vitamin D

For calcium absorption
making bones and teeth

Deficiency causes rickets
bones are soft and deformed

#### Mineral

> Calcium 
> For bone and teeth
> For blood clotting
> Deficiency causes
> Brittle bones and teeth

> Iron
> For making hemoglibin
> Deficiency causes anemia
> No enough red blood cell
> No enough oxygen delivered

> Fibre 
> Foods that contain fibre
> Fruits, vegetables cereal
> Cannot be digested
> Stimulate peristalsis
> Muscle contract and relax to squeeze food along the alimentary canal
> Prevent constipation

### Digestion

#### Digestive system

Alimentary canal
associated organs:
- Salivary gland
- liver
- Pacreas,
- gallbladder

#### Physical digestion

Break up food into smaller pieces without chemical change to the food molecules
- To increase the surface area
- For faster digestion by enzymes
#### Chemical digestion

Break down large, insoluble molecules into small, soluble molecules
- For better absorption into blood
#### Mouth

- Ingestion: taking food and drink into body
- Physical digestion: grind food using teeth

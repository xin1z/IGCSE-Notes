## What is business?

A business can be defined as an **organization** that provides **goods and services** to *others* (customers) who want or need them.

Customers can be **individuals** or **other business**.

### An example: Why is Coca Cola that popular?

1. customer loyalty, which is its unique taste.
2. sponsorship, e.g. FIFA, StarWars etc.
3. iconic packaging, which is highly recognizable.
4. really good supply chain and distribution, which makes buying it wherever, whenever you want possible.

## What is cash?

Cash is **immediately available** for spending on goods and services.

It can be in the form of **notes** and **coins** or **bank accounts** or online payment providers, e.g. Alipay, Wechat Pay.

### Business activities, cashflow

#### Cash inflows

1. sale of products *for cash*
2. payments made by *debtors*, who are customers who have already purchased products from the business but did not pay for them at the time
3. borrowing money from an external source
4. sale of unwanted non-current assets owners or shareholders investing more money into the business

#### Cash outflows

1. purchasing goods or materials for cash
2. paying wages, salaries and other day-to-day expenses in cash
3. purchasing *non-current assets*
4. repaying loans
5. paying *creditors* of the business, which are other businesses which supplied items to the business but were not paid immediately

#### Calculating cashflow statement

$$
net\ cash\ flow = cash\ inflow - cash\ outflow
$$
$$
closing\ balance = opening\ balance + net\ cash\ flow
$$

> **Opening cash (or bank) balance** is *the amount of cash* held by the business at *the start of the month*.
> **Closing cash (or bank) balance** is *the amount of cash* held by the business at *the end of each month*, which becomes next month's opening cash balance.

### Proton number / atomic number:

The number of protons in the nucleus of an atom.

### Mass number / nucleon number:

The total number of protons and neutrons in the nucleus of an atom.

### A table describing the three particles

| NAME     | RELATIVE CHARGE | RELATIVE MASS | WHERE IT LOCATES                            |
| -------- | --------------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| proton   | +1              | 1             | in the nucleus                              |
| neutron  | 0               | 1             | in the nucleus                              |
| electron | -1              | 0             | in the electron shell(s) around the nucleus |

### Period & Group number

**Period Number:** number of electron shells of an atom

**Group Number:** number of outer shell electrons of an atom

### Alkali metals

* low density, so it floats
* soft, so you can cut it with a knife
* low melting point, so it melts in warm water

### Thymolphthalein

A chemical dye used as *an acid-based pH indicator* that transitions **from colorless to blue** within a pH range of *9.3 to 10.5*.

### Outer shell electrons and chemical properties

Some elements have the same quantity of outer shell electrons, which gives them similar chemical properties and bonding behaviors.

### Transition elements
#### Physical properties:

1. high melting point
2. high density
3. harder & stronger

compared with alkali metals

#### Chemical properties:

1. form colored compounds
2. often act as catalysts
3. variable oxidation numbers

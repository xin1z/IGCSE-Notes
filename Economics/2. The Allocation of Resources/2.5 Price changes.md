### An example

#### Question

Using a diagram, analyse the effect on the market if a government subsidises the producers.

#### Answer

A subsidy decreases cost and raises profit, causing the supply curve to shift rightward.

Equilibrium / traded price falls and equilibrium / traded quantity rises.

Right shift of supply &rarr; price&darr; &rarr; downward movement along demand &rarr; shift of supply with the point moving along the demand curve.

### Price mechanism

**Definition:** how a market allocates resources and how prices reflect changes in demand and supply.

#### Three functions of the price mechanism

Higher demand increases price.

| Function                | How a higher price achieves it                                    |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **what to produce**     | acts as a signal to sellers to allocate more resources to produce |
| **how to produce**      | acts as a profit incentive to use efficient production methods    |
| **for whom to produce** | those who can afford the high price will buy the good             |

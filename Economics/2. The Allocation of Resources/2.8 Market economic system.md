### Market economy

Resources are owned and allocated by the market — that is, by consumers (demand) and producers (supply).

Producers aim for maximum profit.

> e.g. no tax, no public firms.

### Planned economy

Resources are owned and allocated by the government.

Producers aim for social welfare. The government plays a central role in resource allocation.

### Advantages of a market economy

| Advantage    | Reason                                                                  |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| low price    | competition forces firms to stay efficient and reduce cost              |
| high quality | profit incentive encourages firms to raise quality to attract consumers |
| more choices | there may be many firms in the market, producing goods                  |

> **Consumer sovereignty**
>
> Consumers decide what to produce, and firms respond quickly to changes in consumer demand.
